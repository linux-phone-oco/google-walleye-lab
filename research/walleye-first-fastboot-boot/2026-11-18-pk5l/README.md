Built by `build/build-pk5l.sh WORK_DIR PK5_BOOT_IMG OUT_DIR` (39 checks, all ok; rebuild byte-identical). Kernel+dtb bytes, header, command line (no earlycon), AVB footer and size equal pk5's.
The init: proc/sys/devtmpfs/configfs/pstore; one gadget (serial walleye-first-boot, product "Pixel 2 walleye (mainline)") with acm.GS0 and mass_storage.0; a respawned `busybox ash -i` on /dev/ttyGS0; a 2 MiB LUN window rewritten every 5 s (uname, cmdline, udc, pstore, iomem, dmesg tail); the SoC watchdog opened and petted every 5 s for 180 s, longer while /tmp/hold exists (`touch /tmp/hold` from the shell), then `reboot -f` (sysrq b behind it).

Porthole's dtb gives usb@a8f8800 (qcom,msm8998-dwc3) with usb@a800000 (snps,dwc3, dr_mode "peripheral", high-speed) and the qusb2 phy.
