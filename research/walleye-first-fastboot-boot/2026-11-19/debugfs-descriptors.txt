### power_mode
UFS current power mode [RX, TX]:gear=[3,3], lane=[2,2], pwr=[FAST MODE,FAST MODE], rate = B

To change power mode write 'GGLLMM' where:
G - selected gear
L - number of lanes
M - power mode:
	1 = fast mode
	2 = slow mode
	4 = fast-auto mode
	5 = slow-auto mode
first letter is for RX, second letter is for TX.
